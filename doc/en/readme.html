Sonos Controller

Sonos Controller lets you control Sonos speakers using NVDA and your keyboard, without opening the Sonos app. It communicates with the speakers directly over your local network. The following features are supported:

  • Selecting a room or group, adjusting its volume, and muting or unmuting it.
  • Controlling playback, skipping tracks, and seeking 10 seconds forward or backward.
  • Announcing what is playing, elapsed and remaining track time, and the battery level of portable speakers.
  • Grouping and ungrouping rooms, and transferring playback between rooms.
  • Playing Sonos Favorites, internet audio streams, and music files stored on your computer.
  • Keeping your own Local favorites and a history of what you played.
  • Creating, editing, enabling, disabling, and deleting alarms.
  • Setting a sleep timer.
  • Adjusting supported speaker settings, including bass, treble, night mode, speech enhancement, and subwoofer level.
  • Controlling shuffle, repeat, and crossfade for the Sonos queue.

Sonos Controller requires NVDA 2026.2 or later. Your computer and Sonos speakers must be connected to the same local network. The add-on is currently available in English only.

How to use the add-on

Press NVDA+Shift+Control+S to activate the Sonos command layer. The add-on discovers your speakers and announces the selected room and its volume. Press the same shortcut again to deactivate the layer.

While the layer is active, use the following keyboard commands. Keys that are not assigned to Sonos deactivate the layer and perform their usual actions. You can also press Escape to leave the layer.

  • Left Arrow and Right Arrow - Select the previous or next room or group. Its volume is announced.
  • Up Arrow and Down Arrow - Increase or decrease the volume by 1%.
  • Page Up and Page Down - Increase or decrease the volume by 5%.
  • M - Mute or unmute the selected room or group.
  • Space - Play or pause. Radio streams that cannot be paused are stopped instead.
  • Control+Right Arrow and Control+Left Arrow - Skip to the next or previous track.
  • Control+Shift+Right Arrow and Control+Shift+Left Arrow - Seek 10 seconds forward or backward.
  • P - Announce what is playing. Press twice quickly to copy the track title and artist.
  • E - Announce the elapsed track time.
  • R - Announce the remaining track time.
  • B - Announce the battery level of a portable speaker.
  • G - Open room grouping.
  • Shift+G - Ungroup the selected room group.
  • T - Transfer playback to another room.
  • F - Favorites: Sonos Favorites and Local favorites.
  • Shift+F - Add what the add-on is playing to Local favorites.
  • H - History of what the add-on played.
  • U - Play an internet radio stream or another audio URL.
  • Control+F - Play an audio file or folder from your computer.
  • A - Alarms.
  • S - Sleep timer.
  • Control+S - Speaker settings.
  • Control+P - Queue settings: shuffle, repeat, and crossfade.

Available commands depend on the playback source. For example, an internet radio station may not support skipping tracks. The add-on announces when an operation is unavailable.

G, T, F, H, A, S, Control+S, and Control+P open additional controls. Press the same shortcut again or Escape to close them. A Sonos command not used by the current menu closes that menu and performs its normal function.

Groups

Press G to group other rooms with the selected room, which must already be playing. Use Left Arrow and Right Arrow to browse available rooms, and Space to select or deselect them. Press Enter to apply the grouping. Playback continues across the group.

Press Shift+G to ungroup the selected group. Playback continues in the original room; the other rooms stop playing and become separate rooms.

Press T to transfer playback from the selected room to an available, ungrouped room. The source room must be playing on its own. Use Left Arrow and Right Arrow to select the destination, then press Space or Enter to confirm. The destination inherits the source room’s volume.

In the grouping and transfer menus, Up Arrow and Down Arrow continue to adjust the volume.

Favorites, addresses and files

Press F to browse your favorites. They are in two sections: Sonos Favorites, which you share with the Sonos app, and Local favorites, which the add-on keeps on this computer. Use Left Arrow and Right Arrow to switch between the sections. Use Up Arrow and Down Arrow to select an item, or Page Up and Page Down to move five items at a time. Press Enter to play the selected favorite.

Local favorites can hold audio URLs, audio files, and folders. To add one, play it with U or Control+F, then press Shift+F. The name is taken from the station when it provides one. In either section, press F2 to rename the selected item, or Delete to remove it after confirmation. A change to a Sonos Favorite is made for everyone in your home and also appears in the Sonos app.

Press H to open the history of what the add-on played: Sonos Favorites, audio URLs, files, and folders. The newest item is first, and the history keeps the last 50. Press Enter to play the selected item again, Shift+F to add it to Local favorites, F2 to rename it, or Delete to remove it after confirmation.

Files and folders in Local favorites and the history play only on this computer, while NVDA is running. If a file or folder has been moved or renamed, the add-on reports that it cannot be found.

Press U to enter an audio URL, such as an internet radio stream. If your clipboard contains a link, the address field is filled in automatically. The add-on supports direct audio streams and M3U or PLS playlists, but not ordinary web pages.

Press Control+F to select an audio file or a folder on your computer. Up to 1,000 audio files can be loaded from a folder and played in filename order. This replaces the existing Sonos queue. The add-on serves local audio to the speaker through NVDA, so NVDA must remain running and the computer must stay awake during playback. If Windows Firewall prompts you, allow NVDA to communicate on private networks.

Some audio streams, particularly Ogg or Opus streams, may not play directly on Sonos speakers. When conversion is needed, the add-on can use FFmpeg. It asks for permission before downloading this free program (approximately 109 MB). The download can be cancelled, and FFmpeg remains installed when the add-on is updated.

Alarms

Press A to manage alarms. Use Up Arrow and Down Arrow to select an alarm. Press Space to enable or disable it, Enter to edit it, N to create a new alarm, or Delete to remove it after confirmation.

The alarm dialog initially focuses the hour field. Press Tab to move between the time, room, repeat schedule, volume, sound, and duration controls.

  • In the hour and minute controls, Up Arrow increases the value. The value wraps to the beginning after reaching the maximum.
  • Page Up and Page Down change the value by 5. Home and End move to the minimum and maximum.
  • Custom duration values do not wrap beyond their minimum or maximum.
  • New alarms are set to repeat once by default.

Alarms run according to the speaker’s clock.

Sleep timer

Press S to open the sleep timer. Use Left Arrow and Right Arrow to choose Off or a preset duration between 5 and 90 minutes. Press Down Arrow to select a custom duration between 1 and 90 minutes, then adjust it with Left Arrow and Right Arrow. Press Enter to apply the timer. If a timer is already running, it opens on the minutes left.

Speaker settings

Press Control+S to adjust the selected room’s speaker settings. Use Up Arrow and Down Arrow to browse settings. Left Arrow and Right Arrow apply changes immediately, while Page Up and Page Down adjust numeric values by 5. The add-on announces each speaker as you reach its settings. For grouped rooms, the list includes settings for each speaker.

Only settings supported by the selected speaker are available. Depending on the model and configuration, these may include:

  • Bass and treble adjustments from -10 to +10.
  • Loudness, status light, and touch controls.
  • Balance.
  • Trueplay calibration, where available.
  • Night mode, speech enhancement, and TV dialog sync on compatible home theater devices.
  • Surround speaker settings and levels, when configured.
  • Subwoofer settings and level, when configured.
  • Subwoofer crossover frequency on an Amp.
  • Fixed line output on a Port or Connect.

Press Control+P to adjust shuffle, repeat, and crossfade. These settings are available when playback uses the Sonos queue.

Supported speakers

The add-on supports Sonos devices that are accessible through local network control, including:

  • Speakers: Era 100, Era 300, Era 100 Pro, Five, One and One SL, and the IKEA SYMFONISK speakers.
  • Portable speakers: Move 2, Move, Roam 2, Roam and Roam SL.
  • Soundbars: Arc Ultra, Beam Ultra, Arc, Beam and Ray, with or without surround speakers and a subwoofer.
  • Subwoofers: Sub 4, Sub Mini and Sub, as part of a room.
  • Components: Amp, Amp Multi, Port and Connect.
  • Older speakers that still work with the Sonos S1 app.

Notes

  • A stereo pair is treated as one room. The same applies to a soundbar with bonded surround speakers or a subwoofer.
  • Portable speakers that are asleep or disconnected from the network may not appear. Reactivate the Sonos layer once they are available.
  • To discover newly added speakers, deactivate and reactivate the Sonos layer.
  • The add-on caches speaker information to speed up subsequent connections.
  • Sonos controls are unavailable on secure screens, including the Windows sign-in screen.

Limitations

  • You cannot browse music services such as Spotify from the add-on. To access music from these services, save it as a Sonos Favorite in the Sonos app, then use F to play it.
  • TV and line-in sources must be selected through the Sonos app.
  • Sonos Ace headphones are not supported.
