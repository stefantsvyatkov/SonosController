Sonos Controller

This add-on controls Sonos speakers from the keyboard, directly over your home network, without the Sonos app. The following features are supported:

  • Choosing a room or group and changing its volume.
  • Play, pause, next and previous track, and jumping 10 seconds back or forward.
  • Hearing what is playing, the elapsed and remaining time, and the battery of a portable speaker.
  • Grouping rooms, breaking a group up, and moving the music to another room.
  • Playing your Sonos Favorites, an internet radio address, or music files from this computer.
  • Creating, editing, turning on and off, and deleting alarms.
  • The sleep timer.
  • Speaker settings such as bass, treble, night mode, speech enhancement and subwoofer level.
  • Shuffle, repeat and crossfade for the Sonos queue.

You need NVDA 2026.2 or later. The speakers must be on the same network as the computer. The add-on is in English.

How to use the add-on

Press NVDA+Shift+Control+S to turn on the Sonos layer. The add-on finds your speakers and names the selected room and its volume. Press NVDA+Shift+Control+S again to turn the layer off.

While the layer is on, the keys below control Sonos. Any other key turns the layer off and does its usual job, so the layer is safe to leave on. Escape also turns it off.

  • Left Arrow and Right Arrow - Choose a room or group. Its volume is announced.
  • Up Arrow and Down Arrow - Change the volume by 1.
  • Page Up and Page Down - Change the volume by 5.
  • M - Mute or unmute.
  • Space - Play or pause. A radio station that cannot pause is stopped.
  • Control+Right Arrow and Control+Left Arrow - Next or previous track.
  • Control+Shift+Right Arrow and Control+Shift+Left Arrow - Jump 10 seconds forward or back.
  • P - Say what is playing. Press it twice quickly to copy the title and artist.
  • E - Say the elapsed time.
  • R - Say the remaining time.
  • B - Say the battery level of a portable speaker.
  • G - Group rooms.
  • Shift+G - Break up the selected group.
  • T - Move the music to another room.
  • F - Sonos Favorites.
  • U - Play an internet radio or audio address.
  • Control+F - Play a music file or folder from this computer.
  • A - Alarms.
  • S - Sleep timer.
  • Control+S - Speaker settings.
  • Control+P - Queue settings: shuffle, repeat and crossfade.

Some sources do not allow every command, for example skipping tracks on a radio station. The add-on then says so.

The letters G, T, F, A, S, Control+S and Control+P open a menu. Press the same key again or Escape to leave it. A key of the layer that the menu does not use leaves the menu and does its usual job.

Groups

Press G to group rooms with the selected one, which must be playing. Left Arrow and Right Arrow choose a room, and Space adds or removes it. Enter makes the group. The music that was playing continues in every room of the group.

Press Shift+G to break up the selected group. The room that was playing keeps playing, and the other rooms stop and become separate rooms again.

Press T to move the music from the selected room, playing on its own, to a room that is free. Left Arrow and Right Arrow choose the room, and Space or Enter moves the music there. The new room gets the same volume.

In both menus, Up Arrow and Down Arrow still change the volume.

Favorites, addresses and files

Press F for your Sonos Favorites. Up Arrow and Down Arrow choose one, and Page Up and Page Down move five at a time. Enter plays it.

Press U to play an audio address, for example an internet radio station. If the clipboard holds a link, it is filled in for you. Direct streams and M3U or PLS playlists work. Ordinary web pages do not.

Press Control+F to play a music file or a whole folder from this computer. A folder can hold up to 1000 audio files, which play in name order. The music replaces what was in the Sonos queue. NVDA sends the music to the speaker itself, so keep NVDA running and the computer awake while it plays. If Windows Firewall asks about NVDA, allow access on private networks.

Some radio streams, often Ogg or Opus, are refused by the speaker. The add-on can then convert them with the free program FFmpeg. It asks you first and downloads FFmpeg only once. The download is about 109 MB and can be cancelled. FFmpeg stays available after updates of the add-on.

Alarms

Press A for the alarms. Up Arrow and Down Arrow choose an alarm. Space turns it on or off. Enter edits it, N creates a new one, and Delete removes it after you confirm.

The alarm window opens on the hour. Tab moves between the time, the room, how often the alarm repeats, the volume, the sound and how long it plays.

  • In the hour and minute lists, Up Arrow gives the next larger number. After the largest comes the smallest again.
  • Page Up and Page Down move 5. Home and End go to the first and last number.
  • A custom duration stops at its largest and smallest value.
  • A new alarm repeats once, unless you choose otherwise.

The speaker's own clock decides when the alarm rings.

Sleep timer

Press S for the sleep timer. Left Arrow and Right Arrow choose Off or 5 to 90 minutes. Down Arrow goes to a custom time of 1 to 90 minutes, set with Left Arrow and Right Arrow. Enter starts the timer.

Speaker settings

Press Control+S for the settings of the selected room. Up Arrow and Down Arrow choose a setting. Left Arrow and Right Arrow change it at once, and Page Up and Page Down change it by 5. The speaker is named when you reach it. In a group, the settings of every room are in the list.

Only the settings a speaker offers are shown. They can include:

  • Bass and treble, from -10 to 10.
  • Loudness, the status light and the touch controls.
  • Balance.
  • Trueplay, when the speaker has been tuned.
  • Night mode, speech enhancement and TV dialog sync, on a soundbar or an Amp.
  • The surround speakers and their levels, when a soundbar has them.
  • The subwoofer and its level, when the room has one.
  • The subwoofer crossover, on an Amp.
  • Fixed line output, on a Port or Connect.

Press Control+P for the queue settings: shuffle, repeat and crossfade. They work while the Sonos queue is playing.

Supported speakers

The add-on works with the speakers you control in the Sonos app:

  • Speakers: Era 100, Era 300, Era 100 Pro, Five, One and One SL, and the IKEA SYMFONISK speakers.
  • Portable speakers: Move 2, Move, Roam 2, Roam and Roam SL.
  • Soundbars: Arc Ultra, Beam Ultra, Arc, Beam and Ray, with or without surround speakers and a subwoofer.
  • Subwoofers: Sub 4, Sub Mini and Sub, as part of a room.
  • Components: Amp, Amp Multi, Port and Connect.
  • Older speakers that still work with the Sonos S1 app.

Notes

  • A stereo pair, or a soundbar with its surround speakers and subwoofer, is one room.
  • A portable speaker that is asleep or away from the network is not listed. Turn the layer off and on again once it is back.
  • Turn the layer off and on again to find speakers you have just added.
  • The add-on remembers where your speakers are, so the next start is faster.
  • The add-on does not work on secure screens, such as the Windows sign-in screen.

Limitations

  • Music services such as Spotify cannot be browsed from the add-on. Save what you like as a Sonos Favorite in the Sonos app, and play it with F.
  • Switching a speaker to its TV or line-in input is done in the Sonos app.
  • Sonos Ace headphones are not supported.
